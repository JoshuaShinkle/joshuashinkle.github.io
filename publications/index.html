Timing Side-Channel Attacks on Mobile NPUs via Edge TPU Resource Contention

Joshua Shinkle, Güliz Seray Tuncay, Antonio Bianchi
Under submission – CCS *

We present the first timing side-channel attack on mobile NPUs, specifically Google's Edge TPU in Pixel smartphones. By exploiting resource contention on the shared Edge TPU, an unprivileged malicious app can infer at runtime which machine learning models victim apps are executing and identify the foreground app. This required reverse engineering Android's undocumented ML hardware acceleration infrastructure from the application layer down to kernel-level interactions with the Edge TPU.

Privacy Risks in Mobile Apps Marketed Toward Military-Affiliated Personnel

Joshua Shinkle, Antonio Bianchi
Under submission – PETS *

We investigate the privacy risks of mobile apps marketed toward military-affiliated personnel by combining large-scale static, dynamic, and network traffic analysis of 242 military-marketed Android apps with a user study of 103 military-affiliated participants (quantitative and qualitative analysis). Our findings reveal systematic data collection practices that diverge from user expectations with amplified consequences for this at-risk population.
