Crash

The easier way to stay.

You’re traveling somewhere new and trying to avoid the cost of a hotel. Chances are, you know someone nearby, but keeping track of where friends live (and whether they’d be open to hosting) isn’t easy.

Crash makes it simple.

Crash helps you stay connected with your friends and agree ahead of time to be there for each other. So when you need a place to stay, you can reach out without second-guessing.

Download on the
App Store

How It Works

  1. Create an account and set your home address.
  2. Connect with friends. Connections are mutual.
  3. See where your friends live.
  4. Need a place to stay? Send a crash request with your dates.
  5. Your friend can approve and share any details for your stay.
  6. Their address unlocks during your stay and is hidden after.

Features

🌙
Crash Requests Send stay requests in seconds. Hosts approve or decline instantly.
🔒
Privacy-First Location Sharing Exact addresses only unlock during approved stays—never before, never after.
🗺️

💬
Messaging Built-in chat with every connection.
📅
Calendar Sync Add stays to your calendar with one tap.
