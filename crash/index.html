Crash

The easiest way to crash at a friend's place.

You're heading somewhere new and don't want to pay for a hotel. You're pretty sure you have at least one friend in the area, but because jobs move people around, it's hard to keep track. On top of that, you clearly haven't talked in a while, so asking to stay the night feels like overstepping. That's where Crash steps in.

Crash is an app that helps friends agree ahead of time to be there for each other in exactly this kind of situation. Crash helps you keep track of where your friends ends up and ask for a place to crash knowing there's no fees, and more importantly, no awkwardness.

How It Works

  1. Sign up and add your home address.
  2. Connect with your friends. Both sides have to accept before either can see the other's location or send a crash request.
  3. See where your friends are living on the map.
  4. When you need a place to stay, send a crash request with your dates.
  5. Your friend approves and optionally adds stay instructions.
  6. Their address unlocks for the duration of your stay and hides automatically when it ends.

Features

🌙
Crash Requests Send a request with arrival date, departure date, and guest count. Hosts get notified instantly and can approve or deny with one tap, with optional crash instructions.
🔒
Privacy-First Address Reveal Your exact address is never public. Friends only see your approximate neighborhood. During an active approved stay, the address unlocks — and disappears automatically when the stay ends.
🗺️
Live Friend Map See where your friends are based as approximate circles, not exact pins. During an active crash, the host's exact location unlocks with one-tap navigation to Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze.
💬
Messaging Every friend connection comes with a built-in direct message thread — real-time and Firestore-backed.
📅
Calendar Integration Add approved stays to your iOS Calendar in one tap. Hosts see "Hosting [Name]", guests see "Crash at [Name]'s place."
