Crash

The easiest way to crash at a friend's place.

Crash turns the awkward "hey can I stay over?" text into a clean, two-tap experience. Request a night at a friend's place, get approved, and pick up exact directions — all in one app. No Airbnb fees. No group chat chaos. Just friends helping friends find a place to sleep.

How It Works

  1. Sign up with your email and set a username and home address.
  2. Find friends by searching their username and sending a request.
  3. Send a crash request — pick the friend, choose your dates, add guests.
  4. Host approves (or denies) and optionally adds instructions.
  5. Address unlocks — the guest can navigate to the exact address during the stay window.
  6. Stay ends — the address reveal expires automatically.

Features

🌙
Crash Requests Send a request with arrival date, departure date, and guest count. Hosts get notified instantly and can approve or deny with one tap, with optional crash instructions.
🔒
Privacy-First Address Reveal Your exact address is never public. Friends only see your approximate neighborhood. During an active approved stay, the address unlocks — and disappears automatically when the stay ends.
🗺️
Live Friend Map See where your friends are based as approximate circles, not exact pins. During an active crash, the host's exact location unlocks with one-tap navigation to Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze.
💬
Messaging Every friend connection comes with a built-in direct message thread — real-time and Firestore-backed.
📅
Calendar Integration Add approved stays to your iOS Calendar in one tap. Hosts see "Hosting [Name]", guests see "Crash at [Name]'s place."
