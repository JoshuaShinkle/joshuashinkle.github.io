About Me

I am a Computer Science PhD student at Purdue University, advised by Associate Professor Antonio Bianchi in the PurSec Lab.

My research interests are in software/systems security and privacy. My research leverages program analysis, reverse engineering, and human-centered methods to study vulnerabilities in mobile phones across both the hardware and software layers.

I've also worked in industry for defense contractors Peraton Labs and Lockheed Martin doing cybersecurity research.
